# Sources

A place to preserve source material used to understand personal context.

Journals, notes, documents, and conversation records may live here or in an external store that better fits the real operating environment. This directory name and preservation method are defaults for the experiment, not requirements.

Content recorded in source material can support facts about the record itself, but that does not make every claim or interpretation in the record a source of truth about the outside world.

Material that is better owned by another system, such as a calendar, email service, or financial service, may be referenced instead of copied here.
