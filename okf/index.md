# OKF research and applied knowledge

This bundle internalizes the operating philosophy and design decisions adopted while working with OKF. It distinguishes the official specification from our own interpretation, and keeps the baseline for the official format in the [version documents](../versions/current.md).

## Operating philosophy

- [Operating principles](operating-principles.md) - Maintain clear ownership of canonical facts together with the context needed to use them.
- [Agent autonomy](agent-autonomy.md) - Prioritize autonomous judgment to make use of OKF's natural-language context and flexible relationship expression.
- [Knowledge internalization](knowledge-internalization.md) - Reflect the meaning and impact of new knowledge in related concepts.

## Reusable patterns and boundaries

- [Source of truth and context](source-of-truth.md) - Centralize responsibility for change while preserving necessary restatement.
- [Context propagation](context-propagation.md) - Update concepts whose meaning changes.
- [Source material and derivatives](external-sources.md) - Separate the responsibilities of source material, extracted artifacts, and internalized knowledge.
- [Knowledge organization for large corpora](large-corpus.md) - Combine core knowledge with summaries and paths for finding supporting material.

## Practical application

- [Adoption judgment](adoption.md) - Distinguish operating models, necessary changes, and scope, and connect them to continued knowledge operations.
- [Learning from adoption feedback](feedback.md) - Select reusable observations and connect them to research and improvements in operational knowledge.
- [Synchronizing research and multilingual editions](distribution.md) - Update language editions while preserving publication boundaries and canonical ownership of shared knowledge.
- [Application guide](../APPLICATION.md) - A default path from target exploration through questions, implementation, and operational handoff.
- [Profiles](../profiles/README.md) - Configurations and application guidance for different usage modes.
- [Templates](../templates/README.md) - Starting points to copy and adapt.
