# Mekra Method operating knowledge

This index leads to the detailed meaning, reasoning, and boundaries of Mekra Method's adopted principles, operating patterns, and adoption judgments. Start with the [operating principles](../PRINCIPLES.md) for the list, core meaning, and relationships of principles, and with the [application guide](../APPLICATION.md) for practical adoption. This knowledge currently uses OKF, while distinguishing [the format and its use](okf-format.md) from Mekra's operating choices. Expressing knowledge in OKF does not limit its reasoning to matters of format.

## Start from a question

| Question to judge | Knowledge and connections to read first |
| --- | --- |
| What should be built or changed now, and what should be completed? | Establish the current state and scope through [adoption judgment](adoption.md), and address unresolved operating directions through [build consultation](construction-consultation.md). |
| What knowledge units and explanatory depth are sufficient? | Compare alternatives through [design exploration and sufficiency](knowledge-design.md), and judge concept boundaries and context to preserve through [knowledge internalization](knowledge-internalization.md). |
| Where should knowledge live, and how are multiple Mekras found? | Distinguish bundles, operating scope, and entry points through [structure and discovery](structure-and-discovery.md). |
| What is needed to use the knowledge and update it later? | Connect the usage environment, material intake, and update methods through [actual use and operational handoff](knowledge-operation.md). |
| What else needs updating when source material or judgment changes? | Confirm change responsibilities through [source of truth and context](source-of-truth.md), and examine [context propagation](context-propagation.md) alongside [epistemic distinctions](epistemic-distinctions.md). |
| With a large collection, what should be internalized and what should be referenced? | Distinguish importance to judgment, evidence, and maintenance cost through [large corpora](large-corpus.md) and [source material and derivatives](external-sources.md). |
| Did it help in practice, and what should inform the method? | Distinguish observations, interpretation, and evidence of effects through [feedback from adoption](feedback.md). |
| What can be published, and how can meaning remain aligned across distributions? | Read [disclosure boundaries](disclosure-boundary.md) and [distribution synchronization](distribution.md) together. |

The lists below provide paths to all concepts. Question-based paths are starting points, not fixed reading boundaries.

## Operating philosophy

- [Autonomous judgment](agent-autonomy.md) - start from the agent's autonomous judgment and use it to guide the application of other operating principles according to purpose and context.
- [Preserving epistemic distinctions](epistemic-distinctions.md) - preserve differences in claims, perspectives, and uncertainty that matter for judgment in the material and the user's account.
- [Operating principles](../PRINCIPLES.md) - use autonomous judgment to maintain sources of truth and context, and reassess the need for structure and guidance.
- [Self-erasure](self-erasure.md) - provide the help judgment needs, and reduce the role of guidance and the method as that need diminishes.
- [Knowledge internalization](knowledge-internalization.md) - reflect the meaning and impact of new knowledge in related concepts.
- [Knowledge design exploration and sufficiency](knowledge-design.md) - compare and choose arrangements and exploration scope against actual use and changes.
- [Order knowledge comparison example](knowledge-design-example.md) - compare questions, changes, and handoff of design reasons through OKF bodies preserving the same rules.

## Reusable patterns and boundaries

- [Source of truth and context](source-of-truth.md) - centralize change responsibility while preserving necessary restatement.
- [Context propagation](context-propagation.md) - update concepts whose meaning changes.
- [Source material and derivatives](external-sources.md) - separate the responsibilities of source material, derivatives, and internalized knowledge.
- [Boundary between information access and external disclosure](disclosure-boundary.md) - distinguish information usable for internal judgment from authority to disclose or transmit it externally.
- [Knowledge organization for large corpora](large-corpus.md) - combine core knowledge with summaries and discovery paths.
- [Role and boundaries of facets](facet-boundaries.md) - use facets as thin lenses on properties of the target rather than presets or a duplicate knowledge layer.
