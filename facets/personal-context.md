# Personal context (preview)

> **Preview:** This facet has not been adopted. Its actual need is being tested in the [research note](../notes/personal-context-knowledge-properties.md) and [experiment](../experiments/personal-context/README.md).

A candidate facet for environments that need to understand and use a person's life and surrounding context over long periods.

The current working hypothesis is to read the same personal context from different perspectives rather than split self-understanding and personalized support into separate knowledge bases.

Questions under particular study include:

- distinguishing a recorded statement from the external fact it refers to
- allowing current state and historically valid state to coexist
- differences in certainty among self-report, observation, interpretation, and hypothesis
- distinguishing information about a person from relational context
- the boundary between information usable for internal judgment and information that may be disclosed or transmitted externally
- the impact of deleting or withdrawing source material or prior judgments on related context

For now, first try to explain these needs through the [Knowledge-centered](knowledge-centered.md) perspective and general OKF principles. Do not require a separate classification system or structure until distinct recurring judgment is observed in real operation.

The minimal experimental structure is in the [personal-context scaffold](../experiments/personal-context/scaffold/). Real cases should determine whether existing facets and general OKF concepts are sufficient or whether this should remain an independent facet.
