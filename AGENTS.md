# Agent instructions

Prioritize the agent's autonomous judgment. The principles below are starting points for judgment and may be applied differently depending on purpose and context.

This repository is the public English edition of OKF research and applied knowledge. It internalizes adopted principles and patterns in [okf/](okf/index.md). Distinguish the official specification from our own interpretations. The [distribution concept](okf/distribution.md) explains the research source of truth and the responsibilities of each language edition.

`profiles/` contains application configurations, `templates/` contains files intended for copying, and `versions/` contains specification baselines and migration records. Reusable conclusions from `notes/` and `experiments/` should be reflected in the relevant OKF concepts and application materials.

Follow the [operating principles](okf/operating-principles.md). Do not expand instructions with generic guidance that a capable LLM can infer from the specification and context.

Start adoption requests with the [application guide](APPLICATION.md), and feedback with the [feedback guide](FEEDBACK.md). During the next synchronization, review direct improvements in this edition for their impact on shared knowledge. The last reviewed baseline is in the [synchronization record](SYNC.json).
