# Agent instructions

Prioritize the agent's autonomous judgment. The principles below are starting points for judgment and may be applied differently depending on purpose and context.

This repository publishes OKF research and applied knowledge. Adopted principles and patterns are internalized in [okf/](okf/index.md); distinguish the official specification from this repository's interpretations. The [distribution concept](okf/distribution.md) explains the research source of truth and publication responsibilities.

`facets/` contains lenses for reading important properties of a target, `templates/` contains files intended for copying, and `versions/` contains specification and OKF Method release baselines and migration records. Reusable conclusions from `notes/` and `experiments/` should be reflected in the relevant OKF concepts and application materials.

Follow the [operating principles](okf/operating-principles.md). Do not expand instructions with generic guidance that a capable LLM can infer from the specification and context.

Start adoption requests with the [application guide](APPLICATION.md), and feedback with the [feedback guide](FEEDBACK.md). Direct improvements made in this repository should be reviewed for their impact on shared knowledge during the next publication. The last reviewed baseline is in the [synchronization record](SYNC.json).
