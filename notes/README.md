# Notes

Keep research, observations, questions, and the background and follow-up to judgments here. The sources of truth for adopted operating knowledge are the relevant concepts and application documents.

Organize each note according to its purpose and content. When discussing judgments or proposals, make established decisions distinguishable from remaining questions, and separate adoption from verification of effects. Link adopted operating knowledge to its source of truth, keeping the background and evidence needed to understand the judgment in the note. When further review is needed, record the conditions for revisiting it or where the work should continue.

For external facts, leave sources and verification dates. Choose headings and order to fit the document's purpose, such as research questions, collected wording, or historical records; common headers or status values are not required.

Content under `notes/` is not treated as current operating guidance.

Cases not yet explained by [adoption feedback](../okf/feedback.md) may also be explored here. Distinguish observed facts from interpretation, and preserve shareable context and the origin of feedback. If an experiment becomes necessary, link the question to be tested into `experiments/`.

A document's existence does not establish that an experiment is running or a conclusion has been adopted. Revisit pending questions when a case warrants it, and do not count judgments already reflected in their sources of truth as still awaiting adoption.
