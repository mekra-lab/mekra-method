# Notes

Keep research, observations, questions, and the background and follow-up to judgments here. The sources of truth for adopted operating knowledge are the relevant concepts and application documents.

- Keep one central question or idea in each document.
- When recording an external fact, preserve its source and verification date.
- Move adopted conclusions into the relevant concept under `okf/` or into an appropriate facet or version document, leaving only the decision result or link in the note.

Content under `notes/` is not treated as current operating guidance.

Cases not yet explained by [adoption feedback](../okf/feedback.md) may also be explored here. Distinguish observed facts from interpretation, and preserve shareable context and the origin of feedback. If an experiment becomes necessary, link the question to be tested into `experiments/`.

## Questions awaiting cases and preserved records

- [Corpus connection strategies and selection conditions](corpus-connection-strategies.md) - compare only the necessary approaches when a real query or update proves difficult with current methods. Implementation and effect validation have not been performed.

- [Knowledge properties revealed by personal context](personal-context-knowledge-properties.md) - adopted general judgments are linked to their sources of truth. Observe remaining personal-context needs through the experiment when material and permitted scope are available.
- [Facet perspectives and boundaries](facet-perspectives.md) - preserve the classification perspective without treating it as an independent taxonomy-building project. Revisit it when actual duplication, discovery failures, or distinct judgment value emerge.

A document's existence does not establish that an experiment is running or a conclusion has been adopted. Revisit pending questions when a case warrants it, and do not count judgments already reflected in their sources of truth as still awaiting adoption.
