# Hypotheses about facet perspectives and boundaries

Concern that use-case guides could be read as presets led to a shift toward a thin `facet` model that describes properties of the target. The adopted role and creation criteria for facets are in [Role and boundaries of facets](../okf/facet-boundaries.md).

This note keeps only unresolved hypotheses about ways of viewing and bounding facets.

## Perspectives currently visible

### Target and environment

Properties that affect what the system is centered on and how it is understood or operated.

Current examples:
- software project
- personal context

### Output and operating character

Properties that affect what the primary output is and what kinds of judgment recur.

Knowledge-centered currently describes environments where knowledge itself is the primary output. Whether it provides enough distinct judgment to remain an independent facet in the long run is still under observation.

### Material characteristics

Judgment that changes because of the scale or form of the source material.

Large corpus is currently the clearest example.

### Control and constraints

Properties that look like publication scope, regulation, or audit requirements may be more likely to become general OKF patterns than facets. The distinction between information access and external disclosure is already handled as the general [disclosure-boundary](../okf/disclosure-boundary.md) pattern.

## Still undecided

Do not turn these perspectives into fixed axes, frontmatter values, or a taxonomy. A facet may span more than one perspective.

Do not create a separate `compositions/` layer either. First observe whether recurring combinations of facets produce independent interaction knowledge.

## Next validation

- Do facet documents add real judgment value without becoming summaries of linked OKF concepts?
- Does Knowledge-centered provide enough distinct judgment to remain an independent facet?
- Can Personal context be handled sufficiently with general facets and OKF concepts?
- If the number of facets grows, is a flat list still enough for discovery?
- Do properties that look like control or constraints continue to become general patterns instead?
